## Repairs to the proposals

- Keep contact mailboxes, petroleum standards, bidding registration and conditional supplier certification from being mistaken for commissioned technology. Preserve genuine planning-software support, portal maintenance and mixed deliverables.
- Retain French interoperability and digital-workplace aliases, Dutch business automation and Spanish electronic-office/NLP work. Context guards distinguish physical equipment and industrial controls without removing the vocabulary. Remove the proposed blanket licence-broker exclusion because broad frameworks can contain application support or a Salesforce resale route.
- Recover UK business-system vocabulary and Digital Outcomes work from an actual work summary. Catalogue navigation, placeholders, event delivery and participant recruitment alone do not establish software scope. Numbered deliverables, wrapped lines and ordinary uses of “location” remain intact.
- Add source-bound reviewed restorations after classification, without caching the override. Changes to scope or eligibility suspend the decision; non-opportunity and explicit no-alternative platform blockers remain. Award, cancellation, withdrawal, deadline and source-delisting rules still apply, including retained related notices.
- Preserve confirmed SAM/CanadaBuys disappearance across unchanged and later snapshots, with its original observation date. A replay cannot revive those records. A verified current CanadaBuys open listing can reopen an unchanged notice despite its older publisher amendment date; stale membership cannot.

## Verification boundary

Verification includes regression fixtures for reproduced failures, the existing labelled relevance benchmark, exact evidence/hash validation, and a baseline/candidate classification comparison against the same frozen retained records and cached English overlays. These are offline checks: no collection, translation-provider or timestamp calls are made by the tests. Population-wide recall is not inferred from the labelled benchmark.
