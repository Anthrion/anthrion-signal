# Signal UI polish — 19 September 2026

## Record and Context screens

- Remove Decision brief and participation-check panels from the inspector, full details and Context. Retain the source facts and evidence in the data; qualification remains a manual source review.
- Show capability tags without quote dropdowns. Keep the main description and the English/Original language control; remove the duplicate original-text accordion.
- Call the comparison page Context, remove the selected-opportunity caption and explanatory banner, and give its current record the existing liquid-metal border. The record text is immediately readable.
- Remove the estimated-contract-value caption from compact result cards. Keep the amount and preserve other meanings, such as award values and framework ceilings.
- Reuse BrandSignature on the main, Context, buyer and supplier pages. Reuse SourceNoticeLink for notice, document, provenance and timeline source actions, with a compact variant. New pages should use these components.
- Remove the buyer-intelligence caption and redundant introductory sentence. Keep the buyer name and collected timeline.

## Markets and remembered browsing

The default pinned order is All, UK, United States, Italy, Nordics, DACH, Spain, Greece, Benelux and France. All is an ordinary configurable item. Groups and their member countries can be pinned together or independently placed in More. Existing custom preferences survive; the formerly implicit All tab starts at the left when old preferences migrate.

The pencil organizer uses drag handles instead of arrow buttons. Mouse and touch dragging show a floating market and its drop position, scroll the list near its edges and save only when released. Escape or an interrupted drag leaves the order unchanged. Focused handles also support Up/Down and Home/End with screen-reader position announcements. Pinning is independent of ordering; moving a market does not change the current selection.

Saved views has been removed. A small, validated browser preference remembers the last search, filters, view and sort. A plain site link starts in the UK. An explicit URL supplies its own state and takes precedence over remembered filters. A reload retains the current URL. Bookmarks, hidden records, language, market arrangement and pane width retain their separate existing storage. Old named-view data is left untouched. Failed or malformed last-view storage falls back to defaults without preventing browsing.

## Buyer and supplier navigation

Buyer names in Context open the existing buyer timeline. Published winners in Context and the main awarded record open a supplier timeline. Nested Back restores the preceding page, filters, scroll position and focus.

Supplier timelines search the collected awarded feed, loaded only when needed. Existing market records are reused. Name-only or locally scoped identities load their country; explicit registry identities can search across markets. They do not claim every contract the supplier has ever won. Explicit registry identifiers support cross-source matching; other identifiers stay within their source and country scope. Without an identifier pair, only an exact normalized name within the same source and country is matched. Conflicting identifiers remain separate. Notice-local ORG/WOR references do not establish corporate identity. Combined supplier labels are not split into invented companies; live records naming an incumbent are not counted as awards. Dates, lots, values and source links remain the published facts.

## English coverage

All countries, including France and the Benelux and DACH members, already enter the private cached English translation process. A global newest-first queue could allow one large country import to dominate a pass. The queue now alternates country buckets, retaining recent-first order within each one and processing a multi-country notice only once. Notice titles and descriptions still take priority over supplementary buyer-name renderings.

The existing approved models, minute/day limits, remote quota reservation, cache validation and per-run budgets remain in place. Completed pairs are reused. Original records stay visible while translation is pending. All is an aggregate of country records and needs no separate translation job. This release does not promise that the outstanding backlog is complete.

## Proposed summaries — separate future work

Start with a compact, consistent brief: what is being bought; the deliverable or lot relevant to Anthrion; and any explicit route, deadline or prerequisite that materially affects a bid. Use structured facts directly where available. An AI model can compress long scope text, but every factual statement should retain an internal pointer to its supporting passage and source revision.

Cache by source-text hash, prompt version and model version. Regenerate when the scope changes. Do not turn absent information into eligibility, assume a Salesforce implementation from an existing-system mention, or infer that a subcontracting role is open. Missing documents should reduce what is asserted. Preserve the full source text and its language control.

Before publication, evaluate a multilingual set spanning live tenders, awards, mixed-scope lots and hardware-only notices. Review unsupported claims, omitted material requirements, incorrect amounts/deadlines and conflation of existing and requested systems. A summary must not decide whether a record is included. Plan its operating allowance separately so it cannot exhaust the translation queue. No AI summaries or additional paid service are enabled in this UI release.
